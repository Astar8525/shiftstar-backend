Ethics & Compliance Policy

Effective: July 1, 2026 · Last updated: July 1, 2026

ShiftStar LLC is committed to conducting business ethically, legally, and with respect for human rights. This policy outlines our commitments regarding forced labor, human trafficking, child labor, and ethical business practices.

ShiftStar LLC has a zero-tolerance policy for forced labor, human trafficking, and child labor in any form — in our own operations and in our supply chain.

1. Forced Labor and Human Trafficking

ShiftStar LLC strictly prohibits and does not engage in, support, or tolerate:

  • Forced labor, bonded labor, or involuntary labor of any kind
  • Human trafficking or any form of modern slavery
  • Withholding of wages, identity documents, or personal freedom as a means of controlling workers
  • Threatening or using violence, intimidation, or coercion against workers
  • Debt bondage or deceptive recruitment practices

We comply with all applicable federal and state laws regarding forced labor and human trafficking, including the Trafficking Victims Protection Act (TVPA) and the California Transparency in Supply Chains Act (SB 657).

2. Child Labor

ShiftStar LLC does not employ, contract with, or support the use of child labor. Specifically:

  • We do not employ anyone under the age of 18 in any capacity
  • Our Service (ShiftStar) is intended for use by adults aged 18 and older
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16
  • We do not engage vendors, contractors, or partners who use child labor

We comply with all applicable child labor laws including the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and applicable Indiana state labor laws.

3. Our Operations

ShiftStar LLC is a single-member LLC operated by one individual in Evansville, Indiana. As a software company, our operations consist of software development, customer support, and business administration. We do not operate physical manufacturing facilities or engage in supply chain activities that involve goods production.

All work performed for ShiftStar LLC is conducted voluntarily, compensated fairly, and in compliance with applicable labor laws.

4. Vendor and Partner Standards

ShiftStar LLC expects all vendors, contractors, and technology partners to share our commitment to ethical business practices. We use the following technology providers who maintain their own ethics and compliance policies:

  • Anthropic (AI services) — anthropic.com/policies
  • Stripe (payment processing) — stripe.com/legal/ssa
  • Railway (cloud hosting) — railway.app/legal/privacy
  • GitHub (code repository) — docs.github.com/en/site-policy

5. Reporting Concerns

If you believe ShiftStar LLC or any of our partners is engaged in forced labor, human trafficking, or child labor, please report it immediately:

  • Contact us: anthonynorman85@gmail.com
  • National Human Trafficking Hotline: 1-888-373-7888 or text "HELP" to 233733
  • Department of Labor Wage and Hour Division: dol.gov/agencies/whd

All reports will be taken seriously and investigated promptly. We prohibit retaliation against anyone who reports concerns in good faith.

6. Compliance and Updates

This policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed to reflect changes in law or our business operations. ShiftStar LLC is committed to continuous improvement in our ethical and compliance practices.
