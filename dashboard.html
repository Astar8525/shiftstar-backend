Welcome to ShiftStar!
Built for the floor. Understood in the boardroom.
Here's how to get started in 3 simple steps.
1
Configure your shift schedule
Click the Shifts button in the top right to set your shift names and times. ShiftStar will automatically detect which shift is active. Set it once — it saves forever.
2
Log your first downtime event
Click + Log event whenever a machine goes down. Enter the machine name, cause, duration, and any notes for the next crew. Takes 10 seconds.
3
Import your production data
Go to the Import tab and paste your CSV export from SAP, MRP, or Excel. ShiftStar will calculate your real OEE — Availability × Performance × Quality — instantly.
