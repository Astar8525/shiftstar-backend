Everything you need to know about ShiftStar.

ShiftStar is a cloud-based shift intelligence platform built for manufacturing supervisors, plant managers, and operations teams. It tracks OEE in real time, logs downtime events, generates AI-powered shift handoff notes, and shows trend analysis across multiple shifts. If you manage a production floor and rely on manual reports or spreadsheets, ShiftStar was built for you.

ShiftStar uses the standard industry formula: OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality. You import your production data as a CSV from SAP, MRP, or any spreadsheet — ShiftStar auto-detects the column names and calculates all three components from your actual numbers. No estimates, no manual formulas. The supported column names include units_produced, units_defect, planned_time_min, downtime_min, and ideal_cycle_sec.

ShiftStar AI reads your actual shift data — logged events, OEE, downtime causes, trend history — and generates four types of intelligence: an end-of-shift digest summarizing what happened and what it cost you, an automatic handoff note for the incoming supervisor, root cause flags identifying patterns in your downtime, and corrective action priorities telling you what to do first next shift. You can also ask ShiftStar AI any question about your shift in plain English.
