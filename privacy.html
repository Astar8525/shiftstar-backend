Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ShiftStar LLC ("ShiftStar", "we" or "us") collects when you use the ShiftStar website and web application (the "Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, company name and role. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it.
  • Workplace data you provide: shift schedules, downtime events and notes, production figures, and the results of files you import (for example from SAP, MRP or spreadsheets).
  • AI Helper content: the shift data and questions you send to the AI Helper, and the summaries, handoff notes and reports it generates for you.
  • Billing information: if you start a free trial or paid plan, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your email address and subscription status. We do not store your card number.
  • Technical information: standard server logs created by our hosting provider (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps), and small pieces of information stored in your browser (see "Cookies and local storage" below).

How we use information

  • To provide, operate and secure the Service, including calculating OEE, trends and reports.
  • To generate AI-powered summaries, handoff notes and corrective-action suggestions that you request.
  • To set up and manage your account, free trial or subscription, and to process payments.
  • To send service messages, and to notify us when a new account is created.
  • To respond to support requests, prevent abuse and comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your production data for advertising.

Who can see your data

Your company's data is kept separate from every other company's data. People who join your company with your invite code can see your company's data according to their role. Company admins can see who belongs to the company and can remove members. ShiftStar LLC accesses your data only as needed to operate, secure and support the Service.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the Service. They process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Railway - cloud hosting and database.
  • Stripe - payment processing for free trials and subscriptions.
  • Anthropic - AI features. The shift data and questions you submit to the AI Helper are sent to Anthropic to generate responses.
  • Resend - email delivery for notifications we receive, such as the name, email, company and role of a new sign-up.
  • Google Fonts and content delivery networks - used to load fonts and icons. Your browser connects to them directly.

We do not otherwise share personal information, except when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer.

Cookies and local storage

ShiftStar stores a small amount of information in your browser to keep you signed in and to remember your language and cookie choice. These are essential for the Service to work. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you cancel a paid subscription, you keep access until the end of your current billing period, after which your account returns to the free Starter plan. After an account is closed, we keep its data for 90 days and then permanently delete it, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, hashed passwords, company-level data separation and access controls. No method of storing or transmitting data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your information by emailing us at the address below. Company admins can also remove members from their company. Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Economic Area and United Kingdom), you may have additional rights, and we will honor valid requests as the law requires. We aim to respond within 30 days.

International use

The Service is hosted in the United States. By using it, you understand that your information will be processed in the United States.

Children

The Service is intended for workplace use by adults and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and we will highlight material changes where appropriate.
